5. Retrofitting Language Models to Operate Over Bytes
URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11111-4
Discussion: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50001234
Summary: Published in Nature, this research demonstrates techniques for modifying pre-trained large language models to ingest raw byte sequences directly rather than relying on subword tokenizers like BPE. Moving to token-free byte-level processing resolves common tokenizer pathologies and significantly improves performance across non-standard character sets and low-resource languages.
